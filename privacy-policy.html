Privacy Policy: Classic Board Games Offline

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This policy explains how the app Classic Board Games Offline ("the app", shown on your phone as "Board Games"), made by Walter Works Games ("we", "us"), handles information. Contact: walterworksgames@gmail.com

The short version

We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not run any servers that the app talks to.

What stays on your phone

  • Games in progress are held only while the app is open.
  • Your wins: the app counts the games you've played and won, so it can show them on the game list. This count is kept only on your phone and is never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it.
  • Your name: if you play with friends on their own phones, you can type a name so they know who is who. It is kept only on your phone (so it's filled in next time) and is shown only to the phones you connect to.

Playing with friends on their own phones

The app uses Google's Nearby Connections (part of Google Play services) to find and connect to friends' phones running the app. Game moves travel directly between the phones. The name you typed (or, if you didn't type one, your phone's model name) is shown to nearby players so they can pick your game from a list. Google Play services handles the connection and is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

If Nearby isn't available, two phones can pair by scanning a QR code. This sets up a direct connection between the phones over your local network. Nothing passes through us.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and it does not collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, if we add ads or online features), we will update this policy and the "last updated" date before the change is released.
